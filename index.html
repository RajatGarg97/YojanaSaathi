Simple scheme helper for families

YojanaSaathi helps families find the right scheme and apply step by step.

First choose a family member. Then choose a scheme. YojanaSaathi shows whether documents are ready and what will happen next.

How To Use

4 simple steps

1 Choose a person Pick the family member you want help for.
2 Choose a scheme See schemes that best match that person.
3 Check documents Green means available. Red means needed.
4 Give consent and apply The app will submit, automate, or show who can help.
